# Interview notes

As the project develops, prepare concise answers to:

1. Why is a random interaction split unsafe for news recommendation?
2. What information is available at recommendation time?
3. Why are NDCG and MRR appropriate for ranked results?
4. How do you handle users with no history?
5. How do you know the hybrid model improves over a popularity baseline?
6. What would have to change before the API could serve real users?

Do not memorize answers before implementing the corresponding components.
